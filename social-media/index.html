Twenty-Five Posts

Twenty-five posts in the Design 3 look: black, wide-tracked type, one brass accent and full-bleed photography. Posts 1 to 5 come from Keith’s founder films. Posts 6 to 15 come from the site’s resource library and the holiday season, from Thanksgiving through New Year’s. Posts 16 to 20 go back to Keith’s films for more of his own words. Posts 21 to 25 borrow encouragement from well-known voices.

Launch posts, 1–5Resources & holidays, 6–15In Keith’s words, 16–20Words to live by, 21–25

The mix

Founder voice, the place, the people and the mission, rotated so the grid never repeats itself two posts in a row.

The tone

Calm, private and premium. Short lines on the image, a short caption, one clear next step, and five hashtags at most.

The rhythm

Post 1 to 5 in order, about three a week. Posts 6 to 15 carry dates where they matter, so the holiday posts land on the right day.
