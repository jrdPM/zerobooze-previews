A Letter From Our Founder

Why I Started ZeroBooze

There’s a moment in everybody’s path. Mine came after a lot of years, and a lot of mornings I’d rather forget. I wanted to figure out how to enjoy life without alcohol in it, and I couldn’t find a place that felt like me.

The options were either clinical or preachy. Nothing felt like a life I actually wanted to live. So I decided to build one: a community, a house on the coast and a brand that says living without alcohol can be the good life, not the consolation prize.

I intend to give back more than I’ve ever gotten out of my sobriety. It’s easy, because it’s the truth.

Wherever you are on your path, you’re welcome here.

Keith PolaskiKeith Polaski, Founder
